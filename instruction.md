## 🎨 DESIGN & BRAND VISION (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

- **Overall Vibe:** Luxurious yet earthy, contemporary yet timeless. Think **Aesop meets the Mongolian steppe**. Clean, airy layouts with bold typography and generous whitespace, with subtle nods to nomadic culture (flowing lines, organic shapes, warm tones).
- **Color Palette:** Deep forest green (`#1a5e2a`), warm cream (`#fbf6ed`), charcoal (`#2c2c2c`), and a **metallic gold/amber** (`#d4a54a`) for accents. Use gradients for depth.
- **Typography:** **Inter** (sans-serif) for body/UI, **Playfair Display** or **Cormorant Garamond** (serif) for headlines — supporting both Latin and Cyrillic.
- **Imagery:** Full‑bleed, high‑resolution photography of Mongolian landscapes, cows, farms, and herders. Cinematic crops with overlay gradients for editorial feel. Avoid generic stock.
- **Motion & Interactivity:**
  - Scroll‑triggered animations (fade‑up, scale, parallax) on every section
  - Smooth cursor‑following effects (custom cursor, orbs)
  - 3D elements (Three.js particles, CSS 3D transforms on cards)
  - Morphing shapes (organic blobs, SVG waves)
  - Micro‑interactions on buttons, cards, navigation (spring physics)
  - Dynamic hero background (video loop or parallax layered scene)
  - Number counters that animate on scroll
  - Scroll‑progress bar as visual accent
  - Text reveal effects (split‑letter, typewriter, gradient‑mask)
  - Sticky elements with smooth transitions
- **Overall Feeling:** Alive, responsive, immersive — like stepping into the brand's world.
